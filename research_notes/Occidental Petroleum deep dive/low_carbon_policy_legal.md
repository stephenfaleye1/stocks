## 1. 1PointFive / Stratos DAC: status, capacity, costs, offtake, DOE hub, Class VI, technology

### Takeaway
As of the last company update (Q2 2026 call, 2026-08-06), Stratos is still not operating. It is roughly 18+ months late against the original mid-2025 start. Management guided that full-plant commissioning would begin around year-end 2026, with operations in 2027, after repairs on Trains 3 and 4. The delay came from a "facility issue" in non-process equipment, disclosed in May 2026, and management says the DAC capture technology itself performed as expected. Stratos has its Class VI permits, its South Texas DAC Hub award was retained by DOE in April 2026, and it has large named CDR buyers. Credit prices are undisclosed.

## 2. 45Q after the One Big Beautiful Bill Act (OBBBA) and 2026 policy

### Takeaway
The OBBBA, signed 2025-07-04, was a net positive for OXY. It raised 45Q for CO2 used in EOR or utilization from $60/t to $85/t, matching geologic storage. It kept DAC at $180/t and preserved transferability. The main new constraint is foreign-entity-of-concern (FEOC) restrictions. Enverus estimates parity cuts conventional CO2-EOR breakevens by over 40%, a direct benefit to OXY as the largest US CO2-EOR operator.

### Cited Findings
- 45Q for EOR/utilization rose from $60/t to $85/t, at parity with saline storage. DAC stays at $180/t for both storage and utilization — [Payne Institute](https://payneinstitute.mines.edu/keeping-up-with-carbon-key-changes-for-45q-tax-credits-under-one-big-beautiful-bill-act-and-possible-impacts/); [Jackson Walker](https://www.jw.com/news/insights-carbon-oxide-sequestration-tax-credit-obbba/); [S&P Global](https://spglobal.com/commodity-insights/en/news-research/latest-news/electric-power/070325-trumps-budget-bill-to-boost-carbon-capture-subsidy-for-oil-producers)
- The final bill dropped earlier draft proposals to eliminate 45Q transferability and to change the inflation base year. It added FEOC restrictions — [Payne Institute](https://payneinstitute.mines.edu/keeping-up-with-carbon-key-changes-for-45q-tax-credits-under-one-big-beautiful-bill-act-and-possible-impacts/); [Imperial College](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/a-z-research/towards-net-zero/energy-transition-commentaries/commentaries/extended-45q-tax-credit-in-trumps-one-big-beautiful-bill--what-does-it-mean-for-ccus/)
- Enverus estimates parity cuts conventional CO2-EOR breakevens by more than 40%, from about $28/bbl to $16/bbl — [Enverus](https://www.enverus.com/newsroom/making-enhanced-oil-recovery-great-again-obba-45q-tax-credit-shift-cuts-ep-costs-by-40); [World Oil 2025-07-29](https://worldoil.com/news/2025/7/29/obba-45q-tax-credit-shift-cuts-e-p-costs-by-40-says-enverus/); [AOGR](https://www.aogr.com/web-exclusives/exclusive-story/45q-tax-credit-parity-significantly-improves-co2-eor-economics)
- The full $85 and $180 rates depend on meeting prevailing-wage and apprenticeship requirements — [Taxpayers for Common Sense fact sheet](https://www.taxpayer.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/45Q-fact-sheet_updated-July-2025.pdf) (garbled in search; verify)
- Critics call utilization parity a taxpayer subsidy for otherwise uneconomic oil extraction — [RBN Energy](https://rbnenergy.com/help-me-obbba-new-budget-law-boosts-carbon-sequestration-and-enhanced-oil-recovery); [Taxpayers for Common Sense](https://www.taxpayer.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/45Q-fact-sheet_updated-July-2025.pdf)
- Inflation indexing applies to facilities placed in service after 2026 (credits stay at $85/$180 for 2024-2026) — [Beancount guide (secondary)](https://beancount.io/fr/blog/2026/05/10/section-45q-carbon-capture-credit-industrial-direct-air-capture-85-180-per-ton-obbba-monetize-sequestration-guide)

### Inferences
- Parity mainly helps OXY's Permian CO2-EOR business, which is its legacy core skill, more than its DAC venture. It turns DAC-to-EOR "net-zero oil" into a $180/t-credited activity, the same rate as DAC to saline storage. This strengthens the incentive critics flag: DAC CO2 sent to EOR earns the same credit.
- At 0.5 Mt/yr, Stratos's maximum 45Q value is about $90M/yr (0.5 Mt x $180/t, if the wage/apprenticeship rules are met), before any CDR-credit sales. This is my arithmetic, not a company figure.
- 45Q survived the 2025 rollback of other IRA credits, which suggests bipartisan/oil-state support. The near-term risk of repeal looks low, but it remains a political variable.

### Gaps
- I found no 2026 legislative or regulatory change to 45Q, and no 2026 IRS guidance specific to OBBBA 45Q changes.
- The 45Q begin-construction deadline (believed to be before 2033 under the IRA) was not verified in this session's sources.
- I found no OXY-disclosed quantification of the 45Q parity benefit to its EOR earnings.

## 4. Legal / regulatory risks

### Takeaway
The largest quantified legal exposure is the Anadarko $5.2B Tronox-settlement deduction dispute in the US Tax Court. If OXY loses, it would have to repay about $2.3B (tentative refund, other cash benefits and interest, as of 2026-03-31). The Andes arbitration was settled in 2024. Federal methane regulation is being loosened, a tailwind. Permian produced-water/seismicity rules are tightening incrementally, an operating-cost headwind. Oman Block 53 is extended to 2050. In Algeria, talks to expand cooperation are ongoing.

### Cited Findings
**IRS / tax**
- Anadarko deducted the $5.2B Tronox settlement it paid in 2015. The IRS disallowed the NOL carryback in September 2018, and Anadarko petitioned the Tax Court in November 2018. As of 2026-03-31 the case was still pending. If the payment is found non-deductible, OXY must repay the tentative refund plus other cash benefits plus interest, totaling about $2.3B. OXY has booked no tax benefit for the $881M tentative refund, so a loss would add little new P&L tax expense beyond interest. Any tax due must be bonded or paid within 90 days of the decision to appeal — [OXY Q1 2026 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/797468/000162828026030584/oxy-20260331.htm); [OXY filing on IR site](https://occidentalpetroleum.gcs-web.com/static-files/9f3250c1-a641-41aa-b04e-b6759a0cd0d9)

## 5. Low-carbon sentiment: critics, supporters, investor valuation

### Takeaway
Critics, including Carbon Market Watch, peer-reviewed DAC-EOR studies and As You Sow, argue that "net-zero oil" via DAC-EOR is implausible and works as a license to keep producing. OXY's framing has been DAC as a path to net-zero or carbon-negative barrels. I found no 2026 evidence that investors assign meaningful value to LCV. The capital roll-off and partner-funding pivot suggest management itself is de-emphasizing it.

### Cited Findings
- Carbon Market Watch (April 2024) calls OXY's CCS/DAC focus a "costly fig leaf for business as usual" and calls net-zero oil an "oxymoron" — [Grist](https://grist.org/accountability/occidental-petroleum-net-zero-oil-climate-strategy-carbon-market-watch-dac-capture-removal/); [CMW report](https://carbonmarketwatch.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/04-24-Oxy-report-1.pdf)
- A peer-reviewed study in IJGGC (ScienceDirect, 2024) finds net-zero GHG oil via DAC-EOR "highly improbable" — [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S175058362400224X)
- An As You Sow shareholder proposal (filed for 2025) sought disclosure of how OXY accounts for DAC removals used for CDR credits vs EOR "net-zero oil", flagging double-counting risk — [As You Sow](https://www.asyousow.org/resolutions/2024/11/21-oxy-disclose-carbon-offset-accounting)
- Hollub's Senate testimony framed DAC as pursuing the "net-zero – or even net-carbon negative – barrel of oil" — [Senate ENR](https://www.energy.senate.gov/services/files/84A01D18-2059-4278-8A46-3BC9509E6307)
- NPR (2023-12-27): OXY invests in DAC "so it can keep selling crude" — [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2023/12/27/1210928126/oil-climate-change-carbon-capture-removal-direct-air-capture-occidental)
- Broader DAC sector signal (September 2026): a climate startup shifted focus from carbon removal to oil recovery, citing a study finding DAC-EOR doesn't deliver carbon-neutral oil — [Energy Connects](https://www.energyconnects.com/news/renewables/2026/september/climate-startup-shifts-focus-from-carbon-removal-to-oil-recovery)
- Supporters/buyers: Microsoft (500 kt), Amazon (250 kt), JPMorganChase (50 kt) and others continue to contract Stratos CDR, which shows corporate-buyer demand — see Section 1 sources.

### Inferences
- With Stratos not operating, a CEO transition away from the DAC champion, and capex roll-off, sell-side and market valuation of LCV is plausibly near zero or option value. OXY trades mainly on Permian/upstream, OxyChem-sale proceeds and deleveraging. This is an inference, and no 2026 analyst SOTP was found.
- Upside optionality: a successful 2027 Stratos ramp at design rates would validate Carbon Engineering technology, unlock the DOE hub and XRG capital, and support licensing. Downside: further delays or underperformance could prompt impairment of the Carbon Engineering acquisition, about $1.1B.
