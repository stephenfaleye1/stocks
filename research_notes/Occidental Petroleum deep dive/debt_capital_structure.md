## Total/net debt by quarter 2024-2026, deleveraging targets, and use of OxyChem proceeds

### Takeaway
Principal debt fell to $11.8B at 6/30/2026. Net principal debt was $7.6B and unrestricted cash was $4.2B. The CrownRock bridge, the $4.5B near-term repayment pledge (met in Q4 2024, seven months early) and the "<$15B" medium-term target (met on 2026-01-02 when OxyChem closed) have all been delivered. The current stated milestone is about $10.0B of principal debt, which had not been reached as of Q2 2026.

### Cited Findings
- **CrownRock financing (closed 2024-08-01):** $5.0B of senior unsecured notes issued in July 2024 in five tranches (2027, 2029, 2032, 2034, 2054). Plus $4.7B of term loans: a $2.0B 364-day loan due 2025 and a $2.7B 2-year loan due 2026 — [SEC 424B2 / 10-Q 2024 via search](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/797468/000095015724001023/424b2.htm); [Rigzone](https://www.rigzone.com/news/occidental_offers_5_billion_bonds_to_fund_crownrock_acquisition-24-jul-2024-177506-article/)

Total repayments in 2024 were $4,507M, including the $2.0B 364-day term loan, $868M of CrownRock 5.625% notes due 2025, and $376M of CrownRock 5.000% notes due 2029 — [OXY 10-Q Q3 2024 / 10-K 2024 via search](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/797468/000079746824000164/oxy-20240930.htm)

### Cited Findings

The 5.000% and 8.500% notes due 2027 appear as repaid, and the 2027 remainder appears to be the 7.000% debentures. ## Berkshire Hathaway preferred and warrants

### Takeaway
About $8.5B (liquidation value) of the original $10B 8% cumulative perpetual preferred remains. The ~$1.5B redeemed in 2023 came through the mandatory $4.00/share trailing-twelve-month (TTM) distribution trigger, and nothing has been redeemed since. Optional redemption starts in 2029 at **105%** of liquidation value, not 110%. Management has signaled redemptions starting in 2029.

### Cited Findings
- In 2023, OXY issued mandatory redemption notices for about $1.5B of liquidation value. These were triggered by "excess" common distributions: to the extent TTM common distributions exceed $4.00/share, OXY must match the excess with preferred redemptions. No preferred was redeemed in 2024 — [OXY ARS FY2024](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/797468/000079746825000056/pro013249_12xoccidentalpet.pdf); [OXY ARS FY2023](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/797468/000079746824000077/occidentalpetroluem_arsx20.pdf)
- At 6/30/2026, Berkshire's Occidental preferred had an aggregate liquidation value of about $8.5B. It pays 8% per year and is redeemable at OXY's option from 2029 at 105% of liquidation value — [Berkshire 10-Q Q2 2026](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/qtrly/2ndqtr26.pdf); [SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001067983/000119312526341032/brka-20260630.htm)
- **Berkshire warrants:** up to 83.9M OXY common shares at **$59.59/share**, per Berkshire's Q2 2026 10-Q. The ~$59.62 figure in the brief is not what this source shows; the strike is adjusted for dividends above thresholds — [Berkshire 10-Q Q2 2026](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/qtrly/2ndqtr26.pdf)
- **Separate public warrants:** 17.9M warrants at a $22.00 strike were outstanding at 6/30/2026. These were issued in 2020 to common holders — [OXY 10-Q Q2 2026 via search](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000797468/000162828026053388/oxy-20260630.htm)
- Hollub (Oct 2025) said OXY would begin redeeming Berkshire's preferred in 2029 — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/10/02/warren-buffett-berkshire-occidental-oxychem.html)

### Inferences
- **Preferred dividend:** 8% × $8.5B ≈ $680M a year, which is comparable to the about $760M annualized interest run rate. Preferred is therefore a senior-like claim on cash flow roughly equal to all bond interest.
- **Redemption cost:** redeeming it all at 105% would cost about $8.9B.
- **Warrant dilution:** 83.9M warrant shares would add roughly 8% to about 1.0B shares outstanding. Exercise proceeds would be about $5.0B (83.9M × $59.59). The 17.9M $22 warrants add about 1.8% more. Share count is approximate and not verified here.
- **TTM trigger:** the $4.00/share TTM test is far above the current common dividend (about $0.24/quarter in 2025-26; not verified here), so mandatory redemptions are unlikely to be triggered unless buybacks are large.

### Gaps
- It is not confirmed whether buybacks count toward the $4.00 trigger. Earlier filings indicate that common "distributions" include buybacks, but this was not verified in this session.
- The exact current OXY common dividend and diluted share count were not retrieved.

### Takeaway
At 6/30/2026, OXY is rated Baa3 (Moody's, positive outlook since 2026-04-24), BBB (Fitch, upgraded from BBB- in February 2026, stable) and BB+ (S&P, outlook positive since May 2026). S&P is the only agency below investment grade, so it is the main upgrade catalyst.

## Contingent liabilities and off-balance-sheet items

### Takeaway
The largest disclosed contingency was legacy environmental remediation (about $1.03B accrued plus up to $2.7B reasonably possible in 2023, concentrated at Diamond Alkali/Passaic). OXY **kept** these liabilities after the OxyChem sale through a subsidiary, Environmental Resource Holdings (ERH), and guarantees OxyChem's indemnities to Berkshire. The Andes/Ecuador arbitration (about $391M award) was resolved in February 2024.

### Cited Findings
- **Retained OxyChem legacy liabilities:** ERH kept legacy tort claims and environmental liabilities tied mainly to historical operations outside the footprint of the sold facilities. Glenn Springs Holdings manages remediation for ERH, and OXY expects to spend on remediation "over many years" — [OXY 10-K FY2025 via search](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/797468/000162828026009059/oxy-20251231.htm)
- OXY guaranteed OxyChem's indemnification obligations to Berkshire — [OXY 10-Q Q1 2026 via search](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000797468/000162828026030584/oxy-20260331.htm)
- **Environmental reserves (2023 10-Q, the latest figure found):** $141M current plus $890M non-current, about $1.03B across 161 sites. Reasonably possible additional losses were up to $2.7B. A significant portion relates to the former Diamond Alkali (DSCC)/Passaic River site, where Maxus had agreed to indemnify OxyChem — [OXY 10-Q Q1 2023](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/797468/000079746823000032/oxy-20230331.htm)
- **Andes Petroleum (Ecuador Block 15 farmout):**
  - In March 2021, an AAA tribunal awarded Andes about $391M plus interest.
  - The vacatur motion failed and a New York federal court enforced the about $392M award.
  - OEPC's counter-arbitration was rejected in July 2023 on res judicata.
  - The parties resolved the dispute in February 2024.
  
  Sources: [Jus Mundi 2021 award](https://jusmundi.com/en/document/decision/en-andes-petroleum-ecuador-ltd-v-occidental-exploration-and-production-company-final-award-friday-26th-march-2021); [Jus Mundi 2023 award](https://jusmundi.com/en/document/decision/en-occidental-exploration-and-production-company-v-andes-petroleum-ecuador-ltd-final-award-saturday-8th-july-2023); [Law360](https://www.law360.com/articles/1796910/andes-oxy-resolve-392m-ecuadorian-award-fight)
- **WES:** an OXY subsidiary held a 1.9% limited partner interest in WES Operating at 12/31/2025. WES repaid its 4.650% notes due 2026 in Q2 2026 — [WES 10-Q Q2 2026 via search](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001423902/000142390226000093/wes-20260630.htm). OXY's main stake is in WES Partners (common units and GP); the size of that stake was not retrieved.
- An unverified blog post describes an OXY corporate guaranty backing the retained environmental obligations — [chemicalsblog](https://www.chemicalsblog.com/en/blog/how-berkshires-oxychem-deal-actually-handles-legacy-environmental-liability)

- The OxyChem sale removed the operating chemicals business but **not** the largest legacy environmental tail. Diamond Alkali/Passaic stays with OXY through ERH. Investors should treat ERH as a debt-like claim, with low billions of dollars of potential exposure.
- **Anadarko/Tronox:** Anadarko paid about $5.15B in 2015 to settle the Tronox fraudulent-conveyance case, before OXY bought Anadarko. This is from background knowledge, not verified in this session. No ongoing Tronox liability was found in 2026 search results.
