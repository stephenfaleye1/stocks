## Q1. Production by basin, 2026 guidance vs actuals, LOE, cost trends

### Takeaway
OXY has beaten production guidance every quarter of 2026 reported so far: Q1 at 1,426 Mboed and Q2 at 1,433 Mboed. The Permian (~804 Mboed in Q2) makes up about 56% of volumes. FY2026 guidance was narrowed to 1,423–1,453 Mboed, around a midpoint of ~1.44 MMboed, roughly flat to 1% growth. Domestic LOE ran about $7.80/boe in Q2 and about $8.54/boe in Q3 (preliminary). The Q3 rise came with a Gulf of America curtailment in September.

## Q2. Proved reserves (FY2025 10-K)

### Takeaway
YE2025 proved reserves were ~4.6 Bboe. PUDs were 1,309 MMboe, about 28% of the total, down from 1,421 MMboe at YE2024, and the Permian holds 69% of PUDs. Reserve replacement was 98% all-in and 107% organic in 2025, with 3-year averages of 154% and 116%.

### Cited Findings
- YE2025 worldwide proved reserves were ~4.6 Bboe. — [OXY 4Q25 release, primary, via search](https://www.oxy.com/siteassets/documents/investors/quarterly-earnings/oxy4q25earningspressrelease.pdf); [Substack analysis of 10-K, secondary](https://bearstone2023.substack.com/p/occidental-petroleum-the-permian)
- Reserve replacement and additions in 2025:
  - All-in reserves replacement ratio: 98% (3-yr average 154%).
  - Organic reserves replacement ratio: 107% (3-yr average 116%).
  - Extensions & discoveries: 340 MMboe, mainly Permian.
  - Source: [SEC 8-K 4Q25 Ex.99.1, primary](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/797468/000162828026009054/oxyex99112-31x25earningsre.htm)
- PUD roll-forward from the FY2025 10-K:
  - PUDs fell from 1,421 MMboe at YE2024 to 1,309 MMboe at YE2025.
  - PUD extensions & discoveries: +247 MMboe, of which Permian 243 MMboe.
  - Transfers to proved developed: –438 MMboe.
  - Purchases: +1 MMboe (Permian acreage trades). Sales: –23 MMboe.
  - ~$2.2B was spent converting PUDs, and ~31% of PUDs were converted when adjusted for revisions and sales.
  - The Permian is 69% of PUDs.
  - Source: [FY2025 10-K, primary](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/797468/000162828026009059/oxy-20251231.htm); [2025 Annual Report via proxyvote](https://materials.proxyvote.com/Approved/674599/20260310/AR_627467/INDEX.HTML?page=42)

### Inferences
- PUD share is ~28% (1,309 / ~4,600). Reserve life is ~8.7 years: ~4.6 Bboe divided by FY2025 production of ~1.43–1.48 MMboed × 365 ≈ 0.53 Bboe per year. This is computed, not company-stated.
- The PUD balance shrank ~8% while the 31% conversion rate is healthy. This suggests OXY is booking PUDs conservatively within the SEC 5-year rule rather than running down inventory.
- A 98% all-in replacement ratio (below 100%) with organic replacement at 107% implies divestitures (asset sales in 2025) offset organic adds.

  - Any price sensitivity of reserves.
  - Oil vs NGL vs gas mix.
  - Standardized measure.
- Inventory-depth claims (years or locations of Permian inventory, CrownRock) were not retrieved.

FCF before working capital stepped up from ~$1.7B in Q1 2026 to ~$3.0B in Q2 2026, the best since Q3 2022.
