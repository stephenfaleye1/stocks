## 1. Who leads OXY in late 2026? Succession, departures, board, and Berkshire's role

### Takeaway
On 2026-06-01 Richard Jackson (an Oxy employee since 2003; COO since October 2025) replaced Vicki Hollub as CEO. The board announced the change on 2026-05-01. Hollub stays on the board, Jack Moore is Chairman, and Sunil Mathew remains CFO. Berkshire Hathaway owns about 26.5–27.5% of the common stock, plus roughly $8.5B of 8% preferred and warrants. Its common share count has not changed for five quarters. In January 2026 Berkshire also bought OxyChem from Occidental for $9.7B.

### Inferences
- The succession was an internal, orderly handover, and keeping Hollub on the board signals continuity. The trade-off is that a former CEO sitting on the board can weaken independent oversight of her successor's departures from her strategy. Hollub has close ties to Berkshire, so this matters.
- Berkshire has influence through three channels: its roughly 27% stake, the $8.5B preferred that ranks ahead of common buybacks, and its role as buyer of OxyChem. It has no board seat and its common holding has been flat since early 2025. The OxyChem purchase, in which Berkshire gave Occidental cash to pay down debt, is the clearest example of Berkshire actively shaping OXY's balance sheet.

## 2. Track record: promises vs. delivery

### Takeaway
Debt reduction has gone faster than promised. The $4.5B post-CrownRock target was met seven months early, OxyChem proceeds drove debt down sharply, principal debt reached $11.8B in Q2 2026, and the next milestone is $10B. Stratos, the direct air capture (DAC) plant run by Oxy's carbon-removal subsidiary 1PointFive, has repeatedly slipped: the original target was end-2024, then end-2025, then Q2 2026, and operations are now expected in 2027. Its cost has risen to about $1.2B. The Anadarko deal is still remembered as an overpayment, and I found no admission from management.

### Cited Findings
**Anadarko (2019)**
- The deal was valued at about $55–58B including debt. Carl Icahn, who held a stake of nearly 10%, called it a catastrophic capital allocation error. Sources put post-close debt at between $38.2B (long-term) and nearly $50B (total). — [Financier Worldwide](https://www.financierworldwide.com/anadarko-sold-to-occidental-for-38bn); [Texas Monthly](https://www.texasmonthly.com/news-politics/how-the-most-hyped-u-s-oil-merger-in-a-decade-went-bust/); [MergerSight](https://www.mergersight.com/post/occidental-petroleum-s-55-billion-acquisition-of-anadarko-petroleum-corporation)
- Within five months of closing, Oxy had repaid $7B of debt. — [Crude Truth Substack](https://crudetruth.substack.com/p/occidental-petroleum-the-company)
- Berkshire's $10B preferred (8%) financed the deal [background, unverified this session]. The roughly $8.5B still outstanding remains a constraint on buybacks in 2026 (see below).
- I found no source in which Hollub concedes she overpaid.

**CrownRock and deleveraging**
- Oxy hit its $4.5B near-term debt reduction target, tied to the CrownRock acquisition, seven months early (reported 2025-02-23). The medium-term goal at that point was debt below $15B. — [Motley Fool, 2025-02-23](https://www.fool.com/investing/2025/02/23/occidental-petroleum-hit-its-45-billion-debt-reduc/)

- Q2 2026 (call of about 2026-08-06): principal debt reached $11.8B, the lowest since Q2 2019, and the immediate priority is $10B. Sources disagree on the size of the quarterly reduction: the 8-K release says $1.9B and Investing.com says $1.5B. — [Q2 2026 8-K release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000797468/000162828026053377/oxyex9916-30x26earningsrel.htm); [Investing.com transcript](https://www.investing.com/news/transcripts/earnings-call-transcript-occidental-beats-q2-2026-estimates-as-cash-flow-surges-93CH-4843829); [Linevest](https://linevestnews.com/article/2026-08-23-occidental-petroleum-oxy-q2-2026-anadarko-debt-reduction-free-cash-flow)

### Inferences
- Credibility on the balance sheet is high: deleveraging targets have been met or beaten, though OxyChem's sale to Berkshire made that much easier. Credibility on DAC timelines is low, with four or more slips and a cost overrun. Jackson owns the DAC narrative, having previously run low-carbon ventures [background, unverified this session], and he now frames it as capex rolling off. That points to quiet de-emphasis rather than abandonment.
- Shareholders hoping for buybacks are in effect waiting until 2029, because redeeming the preferred comes first.

## 5. Internal feedback: employees, safety, regulators

### Takeaway
I found little hard evidence. Employee reviews describe Oxy as stable with few layoffs but slow to promote. The large layoffs date from 2019–2020, after Anadarko. A March 2026 claim of about 1,200 layoffs is unverified. I found no 2025–2026 EPA, OSHA or PHMSA penalties in search. The latest Oxy-specific air enforcement found is a 2022 New Mexico settlement.

### Cited Findings
- Glassdoor reviewers (2026) describe Oxy as the "most stable Oil and Gas Corporation in terms of layoffs" and "Not likely to layoff employees," and also "Slow to promote sustained superior performance." One review cites a 12-year wait for promotion, which is an anecdote. — [Glassdoor layoff reviews](https://www.glassdoor.com/Reviews/Occidental-Petroleum-layoffs-Reviews-EI_IE491.0,20_KH21,28.htm)
- Widespread layoffs followed Anadarko in 2019. The two companies had about 16,000 combined employees before the merger. — [Houston Chronicle](https://www.houstonchronicle.com/business/energy/article/Occidental-starting-widespread-layoffs-in-Houston-14960516.php)
- A March 2026 blog claims a restructuring toward core oil and gas affecting about 1,200 employees, attributed to Reuters. I could not verify this and found no primary report. — [The Retirement Group](https://www.theretirementgroup.com/featured-article/5448064/worst-month-of-layoffs-in-over-a-year-what-if-occidental-petroleum-is-affected)
- October 2022: two Oxy subsidiaries reached a settlement in principle of a New Mexico Clean Air Act citizen suit, with a $500K penalty and a $500K supplemental environmental project, and denied the violations. Earlier items include 2019 EPA chemical-accident-prevention penalties at the Convent and Geismar, Louisiana chemical plants of $121K and $734K, and a $153K Pennsylvania emissions fine. — [OXY 10-Q 2022](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/797468/000079746822000036/oxy-20220930.htm); [10-K 2019](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000797468/000079746820000004/oxy10k12-31x2019.htm); [CHEManager](https://chemanager-online.com/en/news/oxy-subsidiary-pays-epa-fine-of-153-000)
- Legacy OxyChem environmental liabilities stayed with Occidental after the sale to Berkshire. One example is the Diamond Alkali / Passaic River Superfund exposure [background, unverified this session]. — [Insurance Business](https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/us/news/mergers-acquisitions/berkshire-hathaway-to-acquire-oxychem-for-9-7-billion-551826.aspx)
- Violation Tracker aggregates Oxy's penalties. — [Good Jobs First](https://violationtracker.goodjobsfirst.org/parent/occidental-petroleum)

### Inferences
- The OxyChem sale removed most of Oxy's chemical-plant safety and enforcement exposure going forward, but not its legacy remediation liabilities. Permian methane and flaring are now the main regulatory exposure, and that risk is lower under a deregulatory federal EPA.
